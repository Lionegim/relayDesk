# Google / Gmail and Microsoft / Outlook assets

Retrieved from official vendor resources on 19 October 2026. Assets are stored locally and served from Relay Desk; no external image request is made from a user's mailbox UI.

## Sources and usage

- [Google Brand Resource Center](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/)
- [Google API integrations](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/apis/): product icons identify compatible services and functional connections, alongside their names.
- [Google identity branding](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines): the unmodified pre-approved light sign-in button is used only for the Google authorization action. Its supplied English artwork is retained; the accessible action label includes the visible English wording followed by French context for voice control.
- [Microsoft identity branding](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps): official sign-in button and Microsoft symbol.
- [Outlook product page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/outlook/email-and-calendar-software-microsoft-outlook): current FY26 Outlook app icon.
- [Microsoft trademark guidance](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks) and [Microsoft 365 guidance](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/mscle/documents/presentations/FY26_Microsoft_365_trademark_guidelines.pdf).

Gmail and Outlook icons identify their actual integrations in account navigation, connection selection and message/composer details. Generic email channel indicators remain generic because an email can come from several providers. Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 is a single existing connector; no Microsoft 365 Copilot service is implied. The Microsoft symbol is retained as a kit resource; the Outlook icon identifies the mailbox and the official Microsoft button identifies authorization.

Preserve original aspect ratios, artwork and colors; do not apply recoloring, filters, clipping or decorative use. Keep product names nearby and Relay Desk's identity distinct. These proprietary brand assets are owned by their respective companies, are not licensed as Relay Desk source code, and do not imply endorsement. Any future marketing, app-store listing or other use outside this functional integration needs its own review against the linked vendor rules.
