# Instagram and LinkedIn brand assets

Official assets retrieved on 19 October 2026 after the owner explicitly approved acceptance of both download brand-use terms in this chat. Only original, unmodified artwork is served locally. No requests to Meta or LinkedIn are made to render icons in Relay Desk.

## Sources

- [Instagram brand resources](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram/instagram-brand/): official `IG_brand_asset_pack_2023.zip`, linked by Meta's current page. Uses its small black SVG glyph, not a redrawn mark or the 10 MB gradient SVG. The supplied black glyph is a brand-approved variant, not a CSS recoloring.
- [LinkedIn [in] logo guidance](https://brand.linkedin.com/in-logo) and [downloads](https://brand.linkedin.com/downloads): official [in] icon pack, https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/business/en-us/amp/xbu/linkedin-revised-brand-guidelines/logos/in-logo.zip . Uses the blue `LI-In-Bug.png`, preserving its full 635 × 540 canvas and proportions.

## Application

The icons replace text initials in account navigation, service selection/configuration, contact-channel indicators and available account badges in message details. Names remain visible beside controls or in their accessible tooltip labels. Absent contact channels use an explicit dash and accessible absent label; present channels alone show their icon. The contact grid reserves room for all four slots. Original shapes/colors are preserved with `object-fit: contain`, without cropping, filters, masks or colored icon backplates. Images use 32 × 32 containment boxes in 36 × 36 navigation/contact slots; the visible LinkedIn symbol remains above 21 pixels in height.

This is an interface branding change. Instagram remains explicitly marked as a demonstration; LinkedIn stays disabled with its existing availability notice. No OAuth client, API permission, social account, synchronization or messaging capability is added. No fabricated sign-in button is introduced. The exact-preview sending flow is unchanged.

The assets identify channels and are not Relay Desk's logo, an endorsement or a partnership badge. LinkedIn's full wordmark is not included. The downloads do not establish an API partnership or grant permissions beyond their own terms; any future production LinkedIn integration or marketing use must be checked against the relevant brand/API agreements. Instagram assets belong to Meta. LinkedIn and the [in] logo are registered trademarks or trademarks of LinkedIn Corporation and its affiliates in the United States and/or other countries. These assets are not covered by Relay Desk's source-code licensing.
